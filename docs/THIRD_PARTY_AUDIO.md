# Third-party audio

All adhan clips are packaged inside the APK and played locally. Nothing is streamed and no
personal data leaves the device when a voice is previewed or played.

## Default adhan (`app/src/main/res/raw/adhan_local.mp3`)

- Source: user-provided recording `الآذان.mp3` from the local Desktop, copied into the app at the
  user's request.
- File details: MP3, 473,107 bytes, about 204.8 seconds, mono, 16 kHz, with cover art. Stored
  unchanged so the user's own sound is used byte-for-byte.
- Licence: supplied by the app owner for use inside this app; no external CC licence is claimed.

## Additional adhan voices

The person chooses one voice in الإشعارات → الصلاة → صوت الأذان. The choice is stored in
`prayer_alert_preferences` on the device and applies to every prayer.

Every clip below is a **standalone adhan**. The three mosque field recordings are cut at the long
silence *before* any Quran recitation or iqama, and the five reciter recordings are the items named
`Adhan` (not `Adhan with Dua`) from their source collection — no prayer or recitation is included.
Nothing is layered or mixed with another recording.

All eight were trimmed with `ffmpeg`, then normalised in one pass
(`highpass=f=80`, `loudnorm=I=-16:TP=-2:LRA=11`, a small final `volume` trim, `aresample=44100`)
and encoded once at **44.1 kHz mono MP3, 128 kbps**. Measured peak level is about **−2 dB** for
every clip (`−1.78 … −2.02 dB`), so switching voices does not jump in loudness and no clip clips.
The older Makkah and Madinah sources are 16 kHz and 22 kHz; re-encoding cannot add detail that was
not in the source, so the common output rate is for consistent playback across phones, not a claim
of higher source fidelity. The Egyptian source is 48 kHz / 160 kbps.

The short reminder tone (`notification_chime.wav`) is generated by
`scripts/make_notification_chime.py`; it is an original composition for this project.

At the adhan time the full recording plays from `AdhanPlaybackService` with `USAGE_ALARM` and an
exclusive audio focus; the notification itself is silent so the same recording is never played
twice. The optional 5/10/15-minute reminder uses the short chime unless the person explicitly
chooses an adhan for it too.
